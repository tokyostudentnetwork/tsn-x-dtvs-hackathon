Real Problems, Real Solutions

This student-led hackathon is a two-day interdisciplinary challenge in which the problems are not just invented for the occasion, but present novel challenges for participants to solve. Deloitte Tohmatsu Venture Support will supply genuine operational and business problems.

Student teams spend Day 1 defining a business model, and Day 2 building the product. At the end of the hackathon, teams will present their solutions to a panel of judges, who will evaluate the teams based on creativity, feasibility, and impact. Not to mention that there will be prizes for the top teams!

Who Should Attend?

Current undergraduate or graduate students at any Tokyo-area university. Both domestic and international students are welcome. All sessions are in English, with live translations provided.

Team Makeup:

Teams of 4 students with the following complementary roles:

  • Business (1 Member): for developing the business model and strategy
  • Marketing (1 Member): for marketing, finding the target audience, and PR
  • Technical (2 Members): for developing the technical aspects of their team's solution
