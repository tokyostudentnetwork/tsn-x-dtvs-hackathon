Real Problems, Real Solutions

Tokyo Student Network is planning a two-day interdisciplinary challenge in which the problems are not invented for the occasion. Deloitte Tohmatsu Venture Support will supply genuine operational and business problems.

Student teams spend Day 1 defining a business model, and Day 2 dedicated to building the product.

Who Should Attend?

Current undergraduate or graduate students at any Tokyo-area university. Both domestic and international students are welcome. All sessions are in English; with live translations provided.

Team Makeup:

Teams of 4 students with complementary roles:

  • One (1) business student: Business, economics, or strategy background students focused on go-to-market strategy
  • Two (2) technical students: CS, engineering, or data science students or students with programming experience
  • One (1) marketing: Marketing, client outreach, and PR background students focused on business statements, pitch deck narrative, product name, tagline, and target audience definition
